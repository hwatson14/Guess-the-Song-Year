## Catalogue research metadata

Release evidence references public MusicBrainz recording records (https://musicbrainz.org/), with recording IDs and observed dates retained in the expansion ledger. Recording Academy Hall of Fame metadata supplied candidate suggestions (https://www.grammy.com/awards/hall-of-fame-award/); candidate dates were checked separately.

Provider URL candidates were matched from public metadata at https://github.com/madelinehamilton/BiMMuDa and https://github.com/connoraking/Spotify-YouTube-Multiavariate-Analysis (Spotify_Youtube.csv). These datasets supply candidate references, not proof of recording identity. Public Spotify embed and YouTube oEmbed metadata checks are labelled separately. No audio or video is included in this repository.
